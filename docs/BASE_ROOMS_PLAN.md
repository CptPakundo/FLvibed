# The docked windows as HUD 2 pages (the owner, 7 Nov 2026, for 0.4.5)

The order: empty hardpoints and compare in the inventory, more on INFO, then the planet and base rooms at the space HUD's level: dealers built on the inventory page, news and job board in line with it without blocking out the room, the bar refined, every vanilla function kept, K&M and pad alike. Mid-way: the game's item images in every row and in the middle of a dealer (the owner: "text only can be a bit opaque"), a selected mounted item flashing on the ship view.

## Facts learned (FREELANCER_RE "The docked pages" keeps them)

- The dealer's whole stock reads like the inventory's rows: write +0x3A4 = k, call `exe::DealerSelectedGood` (thiscall), restore. The player's rows by `exe::InvSelected` on the partner NN_Inventory.
- `exe::InvSelectRow` works on either window with a row counted from its +0x3C4, off the screen too; selecting one side clears the other's +0x3A4 by itself.
- A window moved off the screen (+4 on its position, vt[0x14] as windowtext's Place) goes on working: clicks by name, the slider, the filter buttons, buying and selling.
- A row's mesh is an image control (vt 0x5E4D7C): position property 6 through vt[0xA8] moves it; five per list, filled for the rows the game's list shows, so the list is scrolled in step (bar at +0x3D0, position +0x3C4). The page therefore keeps five rows. The icons need a hole in the ground and a ground under the game's pass.
- ItemPrice0N holds the game's own number for a visible row; `pub::Market::GetPrice` does not answer a launcher's or a sale's price.
- NN_ShipTrader +0x414 holds indices, not ships: offers are matched by their name to shiparch.ini. ShipTrader3DShip has no rectangle: it follows its window by its own vt[0x14], so after the window goes off the screen a call with x = 0 brings it back.
- Only the dealers' and the ship dealer's windows go off the screen. The job board's and the news' windows stay put.
- The picture of the news and the job board (VideoScreen, VideoFrame) has no rectangle and stays with its window; it is drawn before the UI pass.
- Rich text is placed by +0x330, +0x348, +0x358 and +0x47C of the control, so a window's text can be reflowed into the page's panel.
- The ship view's pulse of a selected mounted item: owner +0x3B4 (short) holds the EquipDesc id, handler 0x4E6EC0, clock `UiBlinkPhase`. Verified live; the pulse keeps running under the NeuroNet pause.
- HD Edition's base infocards already list ships and commodities: INFO adds what the card lacks.
- Scaling the mesh: k above 1 by vt[0x1C] (a 3x3 matrix) makes it vanish; prop 0x18 changed nothing. Open.
- NN_Repair moves off the screen like the dealers (vt[0x14] is ControlSetPosition). A row's tick: toggle +0x3A0, 0 ticked, 1 not. `RepairItemDamageN` (vt 0x5E1924) holds no share to read in any field to +0x43C: an equipment row's health comes from the client's list (node +0x18, `baseinfo::Item::health`), a repeated name taking the next damaged item. The hull's share docked reads nowhere yet, so the hull row has no bar.
- The final stage: `exe::InvSelected` answers on both windows there, each list its own (a row of an empty hardpoint answers 0, the page leaves those out). ShipDealerShipPrice already stands less what went to the dealer (17,020 became 16,900 at a sale of 120), so you pay = that text less +0x9FC of NN_Dealer: 7,600 - 2,400 charged 5,200 exactly. The two lists' same-named controls stand in no fixed order, and a list with no rows has none to compare: the side comes from the +4 parent chain. An empty dealer list once took the player's icons away that way.
- NN_CommodityByBase (vt 0x5D027C, moves off the screen): `CommodityByBasePriceN`, `CommodityByBuySellPriceN`, `CommodityByBaseBaseN`, `CommodityByBaseSystemN`, close button; the inventory closes while it stands and comes back after. In the test saves it listed one base; the 92 visit lines of SaveC701 did not add rows.
- The inventory's game window stands behind that page: a hole in its ground shows the window. The jettison panel's mesh therefore stands below the window (0.715 H). TradeRouteButton stood in the card's hole and now stands off the screen.
- ShipView_Control (Inventory +0x330) is the same class as ShipTrader3DShip (vt 0x5E4BCC), but its builder 0x478100 clears its visible bits, and with it stand ShipView_Toggle, ShipView_DamageBar, ShipView_Name, Shipview_Frame (fetched in 0x47B410). What sets a ship on that class was not found in the time given. The status panel's view (hudvis) needs the ship in space.

- The room's words cover the art and do not hide it: `+0x84` on the room buttons is unsafe, the buttons are made anew per room (a kept `Win::own` entry would write to freed memory on restore) and the icon button's pose setter is not a signed one (`Repose` refuses it). State bit 1 is not an option: `room::InScene` and `NavBusy` read it. So no game write at all: `ui_style 0` needs no restore.
- The click box (`FLvibedHud2Hot`, top level) stands over the NavBar in the game's hit test: `nnstrip hit 640 30` answers the box with the words up, ButtonControl3 with `ui_style 0`.
- The click box stood down under every base page until now: `skinchip::Frame` was given the strip's standing only, and a page has no strip. The page's rows and chips had no mouse route; they ride on the room's words now. Hit test confirms the box on a row; a real mouse is still not tried.
- A room's word is its hotspot's label; an action's is the game's hint (`baseinfo::HintText`), read once per label. A room's hint names more than the room.
- The words shrink to the text floor to fit; when they still do not leave the strip 0.30 H and clear the NeuroNet's plate, the old look (icons on glass) stands. 4:3 is expected to fall there; not run.
- The pages' top: `max(0.165 H, strip's or words' foot + 0.006 H)`. 0.165 H stays the least, and the ship dealer's view and its texts stay on that line, because the game's 3D ship does not move.
- FLUF: its windows draw over the words as over the strip; the words do not fade under them (the opaque plates have no dim step). Not run with FLUF.

## Not tried

A strip of three lines over a page (the only page without a window flag is best prices; the top's rule is read, not seen); a mod's room with more words than the bar holds; real mouse (clicks, wheel, chips, the swallow of a click on blank page: nothing is under it, the game's controls stand off the screen); real pad; multiplayer; 4:3. Best prices with more than one base (its scrolling, whether the game's list has a bar of its own); a final stage where the player cannot pay, or where items do not fit, in game (only sold by hand); mounting at the final stage (left out: the rows are the new ship's copy, a mount there unread); typing the jettison number (JettisonQuantityEdit did not answer as visible in the test, the page has no field for it); the docked ship view; a ship with the hull alone damaged. With `base_pages` set to 0 while a page stands the window comes back with its icons out of place until it is opened again. "Yes" in the prices' BASE SELLS column is read by its first letter: another language shows no place to buy.
