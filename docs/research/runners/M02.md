## Plan

Ordered by expected gain per effort; each lever names the segment it moves and the expected seconds against the PB
(1,098.7 s; par 876).

   within 5 km of King (`nag_battle3`). Time the 4th escort's death ourselves (deterministic, unlike the runners'
   NPC finish): bring it low, start the cruise charge, and kill it (or let the cruise begin the instant it dies) so that
   at the spawn we are <= 1-2 km off and moving at cruise on the spawn -> run-point heading (-22099, 0, 75896). Then
   target Ashcroft (the marked ship, not the navmarker), match his line at 110-230 m, engine-kill coast to fire guns +
   Javelin, re-cruise when the coast decays (~4 s, 297 -> 283 m/s), repeat; stop firing at the eject; tractor. Expected
   pod 115 -> 25-35 s (runners 18-29) = -80..-90; cost: ashcroft 25 -> 35-46 s (+10..+20, inside the 26 s we are under
   par) and nygatein +35..+45 s (the pod 10-13 km farther from the NY gate). Net -45..-70 s [I from the measured parts].
   First probe: from fixture S418 (unverified) / a save before "Destroy the Enemy Rogues", log the start geometry

   the 6th Pueblo kill to cruise toward Transport1 (r1 / r3 cruise the last 24-25 s before the banner), then go
