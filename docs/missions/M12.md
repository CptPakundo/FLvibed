# M12 (St02 story mission) - tester runbook

Fly into the Nomad Lair (system St02) with Juni and King, take a power cell from the core, break out, jump to St01 and land on Toledo. Read-only analysis of the script; nothing in the game was changed.

Source: `Install\Freelancer HD Edition\DATA\MISSIONS\M12\`
- `m12.ini` (3574 lines, 138 triggers, 168 mission ships, 36 formations). Ship/formation blocks are lines 1-1851; triggers start at 1904.
- `npcships.ini` (ship archetypes only: King and Juni `or_elite`, Von Claussen `rh_elite`, Nomad fighters `no_fighter` d12, gunboat `no_gunboat` d16, battleship d18 defined but never spawned; no triggers).
- `m012_s071a_st02_01_offer.ini` (offer scene, 19 lines) and `m012_s071d_st02_01_reoffer.ini` (re-offer scene, 20 lines). Both are at `St02_01_base`, Bar, with King and Juni, `autoplay`, and `relocate_player = deck, St02_01_base`.
- `dummy_test.ini` (11 lines): a test scene at `Li01_15_base` Deck with King. `start` (2010) adds it as an RTC. It is a leftover; nothing else uses it.
- `M12_Osiris.thn`: the camera cutscene around the Battleship Osiris with a cloak effect (called at 2153).

**Conventions** (same as M01a)
- Line numbers are 1-based (as an editor shows them). `->` = `Act_ActTrig`.
- Times are seconds. `Cnd_Timer` values add up along a chain. Comm line lengths come from the `sec` column of `subtitles\data\subtitles.tsv` and are written `[x.xx]`.
- Triggers without `InitState` are inactive until something activates them (`start` at 2007 is the only `InitState = ACTIVE`).
- Verified in M01a (not re-verified here): `Act_NagDistLeaving` / `Act_NagDistTowards` escalate to a mission FAIL.
- Ids are resolved with `research\strings\resources.tsv` (titles only).
- Many `Act_SetVibe*`, `Act_Invulnerable`, `Act_GiveObjList` actions only set AI behaviour. They are not listed one by one.

**Start:** Battleship Osiris, base `st02_01_base`, room Bar. King offers the mission (title `24200` "MISSION 12", offer text `24205`). The previous mission (M11) hands over: `start` (2004) waits for the base `Li01_15_base` and sets friend reputation with the Order, Lib. Police, LSF and Navy (2013-2016). Then `lce_bar_li01_15` (2019, bar of `Li01_15_base`) re-adds the M11 normal scene and arms `bse_St02_01` (2025, entering `st02_01_base`), which arms `lce_bar_St01_02` (2030, entering the Bar there). That sets the offer.

**End:** SUCCEED when the player leaves space (docks) at Toledo, `St01_01_Base`, in system St01, after getting within 3000 m of `St01_dock_ring_1`. Reward 10000 credits and the power cell is removed (3554-3560).

## Walkthrough

### Step 0 - Osiris bar: accept the job

- **Starts:** the player is at `st02_01_base`. `lce_bar_St01_02` (2030) runs on entering the Bar: offer scene `M012_s071a_St02_01_offer.ini` added (2033), title `24200`, offer `24205`, arms `mrp_accept` (2036).

- **Blocking UI / scenes:** the offer scene (King and Juni, `autoplay`, moves the player to the Deck). Offer dialog: needs `click OfferDialogAcceptButton`. `Close-FLBlocker` does not decide accept or decline.
- **Required action:** talk to King, accept. Harness: `talk story` (or `talk mission`) then `click OfferDialogAcceptButton`.
- `mrp_accept` (2046, `Cnd_MsnResponse accept`): removes the re-offer scene (2050), deactivates `mrp_reject` (2051), no random population (2052), arms `lcx_bar_St02_01` (2053).

- **Fail paths:** none. **Safe window:** yes, anything before accepting is harmless.
