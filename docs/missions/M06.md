# M06 (Br05 Dublin / Br04 Leeds) - tester runbook

Mission 6: race Dexter Hovis around a course at Battleship Hood, follow him to the Station Glorious defence, escort Quintaine through an ambush and a jump hole to Planet Leeds.

Sources (all under `Install\Freelancer HD Edition\DATA\MISSIONS\M06\`, read-only analysis):
`m06.ini` (triggers), `npcships.ini` (ship archetypes only: no triggers), the base scenes `m006_s032a_br05_01_offer.ini` (Hood bar, autoplay), `m006_s032d_br05_01_reoffer.ini` (Hood bar, after Reject), `m006_s033x_br05_01_nrml.ini` (Hood Deck2, Dexter, autoplay, `relocate_player = deck`), `m006_s034x_br05_02_nrml.ini` (Glorious Deck2, Quintaine, autoplay, `relocate_player = deck`). The six `m06_Race_Hood*.thn` / `M06_*.thn` files are camera scenes called with `Act_CallThorn`; `m06_Race_Hood3.thn` is never called by the script.

**Conventions** (as M01a)

- Line numbers are 1-based (as an editor shows them).
- `->` = `Act_ActTrig`. Times are seconds. `+lines` = unknown extra comm time; where the line id is a `DX_` id I give its `sec` from `subtitles\data\subtitles.tsv` and add it up.
- Triggers without `InitState` are inactive until activated. Only `tr_start` (330) is `InitState = ACTIVE`.
- Ids are resolved from `research\strings\resources.tsv` (titles only). Nags (`Act_NagDist*`) are treated as FAIL warnings: verified in M01a that they escalate to the given text. Every nag text of M06 starts with "Mission Failed" (they are FAIL strings). Timing of the escalation is engine-side (not in the script): **Uncertain**, measure it.
- `bool_2ndrace` (2325) and `bool_cheating` (1626) are used as boolean flags: a trigger that is activated (state ON/ACTIVE) and read with `Cnd_WatchTrigger`. `bool_2ndrace` ON = the second race. `bool_cheating` ACTIVE = Dexter cheated (his wingmen and the platforms attack). Nothing in the file fires them (`Cnd_Destroyed Player 1`).
- Locking: `Act_PlayerCanDock = false, <base>` and `Act_PlayerCanTradelane = false, <ringA>, <ringB>` (list form) are used right before the player must dock / use that target (2393, 2457, 2776, 3368, 3439, 3441). **Uncertain** (as in M01a): by the pattern the list form names the allowed target and the no-argument form (366, 543, 753, 2376, 2408, 3018, 3426) blocks docking everywhere. Check `Get-FLStoryState` before assuming a dock works.

**Start:** Docked at Planet Leeds (Br04_01), where M05 ends (M05 `mission_succeed` fires on leaving the Equipment room). Fly to Dublin (Br05) and land on Battleship Hood (`Br05_01`, base `Br05_01_base`, "Land on Battleship Hood" 23030). In the Hood bar Dexter Hovis offers the job (`m006_s032a`, autoplay).

**End:** Docking at Planet Leeds (`Br04_01_base`) fires `mission_completed` (3549): +7000 credits (3551), `ChangeState SUCCEED` (3554), jump-hole locks released. The player is then docked on Leeds, the next mission (M07) is offered there.
