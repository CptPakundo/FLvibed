# M03 (Li02 story mission 3) - tester runbook

Escort a convoy through the Barrera Passage to Research Station Willard, then help defend Willard against Rheinland ships. Read-only analysis; nothing in the game was changed.

**Two ways to run it:** `controller\tools\missions\M03.ps1` (the harness drives every step) and
`controller\tools\missions\M03objective.ps1` (the autopilot's objective mode flies every space part from the
objectives alone; the harness only launches from Willard and opens the CA Minor bar - "Mission Result: Success" 19 Oct
2026; sweep section `objective-M03`). What the objective mode needed here: the lane objective rides 42 -> 43 (the
mission's lane permission), and "Escort the Transport Convoy to the First Buoy" means formation with transport1 (the
ship "Fly to the Transport Convoy" named), not flying to the buoy (>1200 m from transport1 fails, 22560).

Source: `Install\Freelancer HD Edition\DATA\MISSIONS\M03\m03.ini` (2788 lines), `npcships.ini` (ship loadouts only, no triggers), and the base scenes `m003_s012xa_li01_01_nrml.ini`, `m003_s012xb_li01_01_nrml.ini`, `m003_s014a_li02_02_offer.ini`, `m003_s014d_li02_02_reoffer.ini`, `m003_s015x_li02_02_nrml.ini`. Other files in the folder: `M03_Rfighter_*.thn` and `m03_Survivor1-3.thn` (space camera scenes, binary, not read).

**Conventions**

- Line numbers are the ones the Read tool printed for this file. They are 1-based (`[Mission]` is line 1). The number of a trigger is its `nickname` line.
- `->` = `Act_ActTrig`. Times are seconds. `+lines` means a comm line adds its duration. Durations come from the `sec` column of `subtitles\data\subtitles.tsv`.
- Triggers without `InitState` are inactive until activated. Only `init_m03` (936) starts active.
- Ids are resolved with `research\strings\resources.tsv`. Only titles are quoted.
- **Nags escalate to FAIL** (verified in M01a). Here every `Act_NagDistLeaving` / `Act_NagDistTowards` carries a FAIL text id (22560, 22565, 22570, 22575, 22580, 22585, 22587, 22549, 22590). So the nag text equals the FAIL text for all of them, and every nag is a FAIL path. The script gives no nag times; the engine decides (**Uncertain**).
- The mission has two kinds of comms. `Act_EtherComm` / `Act_SendComm` lines are the ones in the `comm` event.
- Harness names: see `README.md`. Comm hashes: `Get-FLHash 'DX_M03_0267_JUNI'`.

**Mission texts**: title 22400 "MISSION 3"; offer text 22405 (also `mission_text_id` in the offer scenes, `m003_s014a...offer.ini`). Offer text changes on progress: 22406 at Willard dock (2031), 22407 at the Walker save (2467).
