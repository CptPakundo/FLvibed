# M05 (Br03 Cambridge / Bw01 Omega-3 / Br04 Leeds, story mission 5) - tester runbook

Source: `Install\Freelancer HD Edition\DATA\MISSIONS\M05\m05.ini` (2218 lines), `npcships.ini`, and the base scenes
`m005_s026a_br03_01_offer.ini`, `m005_s026d_br03_01_reoffer.ini`, `m005_s027xa/xb/xc_bw01_01_nrml.ini`,
`m005_s028x_bw01_05_nrml.ini`, `m005_s029x_br04_01_nrml.ini` (read-only analysis, nothing in the game was changed).

**Objective mode script:** `controller\tools\missions\M05objective.ps1` (from Save1d87.fl): the autopilot's objective
mode flies every space part; the rooms by `Complete-FLBaseStory` (Sprague's scene chain, Baxter's bar) and the Tobias
scene at Leeds. Sweep section `objective-M05`.

**Conventions**

- Line numbers are 1-based (editor numbers).
- `->` = `Act_ActTrig`. Times are seconds. `+lines` = comm line durations; they are given here from the `sec` column of `subtitles\data\subtitles.tsv` (DX_ ids). Lines of one chain are assumed to play one after the other.
- Triggers without `InitState = ACTIVE` are inactive until something activates them (as in M01a). Only `tr_offer` (655) has `InitState`.
- **Nags escalate to FAIL** (verified in M01a). Every nag id in M05 is itself a "Mission Failed." string, so **nag text = FAIL text** for all of them. The nag delay is engine-internal: this script gives no seconds (`NAG_ALWAYS`, distance args). Treat each nag as "will fail the mission if the player ignores the objective for a while". **Uncertain:** the delay.
- Ids are resolved from `research\strings\resources.tsv` (titles only). Systems: Br03 = Cambridge, Bw01 = Omega-3, Br04 = Leeds. Sprague = `Bw01_01` (base `Bw01_01_base`, dock ring `bw01_01_dock_ring_1`). Baxter Research Station = `Bw01_05`. Leeds = `Br04_01`.
- Mission NPCs: Juni (216504, the player's partner, label `lbl_friends`), Sinclair (216505, appears at Sprague), two Rheinland traitors "Holger Schmidt" / "Jochen Wiedemann" (216508/216509, label `lbl_traitors`), Battleship Wotan (216502, `rh_battleship`, label `lbl_battleship`).

**Start:** Cambridge (`Br03_01_base`), room **Bar**. Juni offers the mission (autoplay scene `s026a`). The script has no hand-over from the previous mission: `tr_offer` is active from the start (654-659). **Uncertain:** how M04 ends and where the player is (the M04 runbook owns that).

**End:** the player docks at Leeds (`Br04_01_Base`), the autoplay scene `s029x` (Tobias, room Equipment) runs, the player is relocated to Cityscape, i.e. leaves Equipment -> `mission_succeed` (2206) -> SUCCEED. Reputation is set (Liberty, LSF and Liberty Police -0.9, Bounty Hunters -0.7, Bretonia 0). The player is afterwards in Leeds, room Cityscape.
