# M02 (Li01 / Li03 story mission 2, "Capture the Artifact smuggler") - tester runbook

Source: `Install\Freelancer HD Edition\DATA\MISSIONS\M02\m02.ini` (triggers), `npcships.ini` (ships), base scenes
`m002_s009a_li01_01_offer.ini`, `m002_s009d_li01_01_reoffer.ini`, `m002_s010x_li01_01_nrml.ini`,
`m002_s011x_li01_03_nrml.ini`. Read-only analysis; nothing was run in the game.

**Conventions**

- Line numbers are the ones the Read tool printed for `m02.ini` (1-based: `[Mission]` is line 1).
- `->` = `Act_ActTrig`. `+lines` = time of comm lines; here converted with the `sec` column of `subtitles.tsv` (DX ids).
- Dialog lines `line = A, B, id, n, m`: the `n` / `m` fields are not documented. **Uncertain**: `n` looks like the author's
  length estimate and `m` a delay (for example `, 3, 2`). Durations below use `sec` only. Lines with `m` > 0 add an
  unknown gap (0780 KING etc. have `9, 5`; `0170` in `leaving_second` has `4, 9`).
- Triggers without `InitState` are inactive until another trigger activates them. Only `key_offer` (539) is `ACTIVE`.
- Only `Act_ChangeState` fixes the outcome. **Nags** (`Act_NagDistTowards` / `Act_NagDistLeaving`) escalate to the
  given text as a FAIL (verified in M01a). Nag ids here are the FAIL texts (see the outcomes table). The nag timing is
  engine-defined, not in the file.
- Resolved texts (titles only) come from `research\strings\resources.tsv`.

**Verified 18 Oct 2026 (controller\tools\missions\M02.ps1, no god mode, 1125 s):** step 8 holds at the waypoint and
scans every ship, the mission's marked ship (Cosmo, `mark id=`) first - the server's `scan` event confirms it and the
objective follows; step 14's fight must stay with King (escort, no fleeing: leaving fails "You ran from the battle");
the Colorado gate refuses docking while Battleship Unity's dialogue runs (wait for it); Pittsburgh / Pueblo lanes: a
refused lane entry is recovered by travel.
