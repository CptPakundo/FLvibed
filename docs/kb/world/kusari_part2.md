## 1. Overview
This chunk is Kusari's inner politics seen from the frontier. Three themes run through it. (a) A **technology race**:
Kusari wants the alien Jump Gate / Trade Lane / superconductor know-how Liberty monopolises; the two keiretsu run
rival programmes (Kishiro at Kansai, Honshu; Samura at Nansei, Kyushu) while the government funds a doomed-looking
**Chugoku Gate** in Hokkaido that several voices say is a pretext to get a gate to take apart. (b) **Insurgency and
proxy war**: the Blood Dragons (old royalist guard turned rebels), the Golden Chrysanthemums (women's revolutionary
cult fuelled by Cardamine) and the Farmers Alliance (dispossessed Kyushu farmers) are all funded or steered by
someone (GMG, Samura, an unnamed Shinjuku patron), while the Hogosha mafia, bribed bureaucrats and the Corsairs /
Outcasts of the Edge muddy everything. (c) **Xenophobic border management**: Shikoku is the single buffer system
where Gaijin (Liberty, Border Worlds) are allowed, policed by State Police, Navy battleships, Samura convoys and the
Bounty Hunters Guild; resentment of foreigners, prison, fish farms and commodity routes fill the micro detail. A large
share of the entries are trade-route hints (what to carry where) in the guise of small talk.

## 2. Factions

### Lawful / state
- **Kusari government (New Tokyo)**: orders the Chugoku Gate (#133363, #133371), keeps the Navy thinly spread
  (#133354, #133348), forbids foreign Bounty Hunters in Honshu on bushido grounds (#133352), offers corporate
  incentives for Kyushu from 400 AS (#133527, #133581), levied a "reparation" tax on the GMG for Eighty Years' War
  loans / gate costs (#133413, #133427), pays Samura an annual research subsidy (#133578, #133548). Bureaucrats are
  repeatedly called bribable by Samura, the Hogosha and the Farmers' patron (#133356, #133371, #133377, #133415,
  #133434, #134843). Kusari bans domestic Side Arm production (#133484).
- **Kusari Naval Forces**: obsolete "Edo" classes of old Samura design (#133320); want a new fleet (#133320, #133329);
  battleships Myoko (Shikoku), Nagumo (Kyushu), Matsumoto (Hokkaido). Heavy Fighter sold only at dealers on Kusari
  battleships (#393419). Nagumo guards the Nansei complex (#133565); its commander ordered Golden C. summarily killed
  (#133553). Lost fighters / wrecks around Kansai and Hokkaido (#134333, #134336).
- **Kusari State Police**: proud of honesty (#133356, #133415), under-resourced; Akita (Honshu) and Ohashi (Shikoku),
  Kyushu east half (#133529). Patrols the Hiryo Cloud, 50 percent casualties (#133351).
- **Bounty Hunters Guild**: only allowed a base in Shikoku (Deshima) (#133454, #133463); paid by Samura against
  Blood Dragons (#133464), by IC / Synth Foods against the Farmers (#133467, #133470), by Universal (#133469), plus a
  standing government bounty on Fuchu escapees (#133471). Police despise them (#133446, #133494) but wish for them in
  Honshu (#133352) and Sapporo (#133415).
- **Liberty presence**: Deep Space Engineering (DSE) crews build the Chugoku Gate and alone handle gate parts
  (#133393-#133402, #133408, #133409); Ageira black boxes (#133398); Liberty IP rules protect them (#133401); IC will
  not insure (#133397). A DSE hand names LSF / company policy (#133398).

### Corporations
- **Samura** ("old lady of Kusari", #133345): government-backed, hires staff for life (#133550), runs Kyushu food
  collectives (#133523), Nansei research (#133565), Hokkaido gas expansion (#133412), transport and supply convoys in
  Shikoku (#133518, #133519), Deshima / Ohashi supply. Accused of: stealing building supplies from competitors and
  bribing New Tokyo police (#133322), funding the Farmers Alliance (#133483, #133587, #134843, #133579), running the
  Blood Dragons' enemies' interests, a kickback structure around its subsidy (#133578). Partner of the Hogosha
  (#133582). Chugoku Gate is Samura's lever against the GMG (#133406-#133407).
- **Kishiro** (second keiretsu): modern, better paid, more creative scientists (#133335, #133574); Kansai programme;
  Yukawa Shipyard builds its gas miners, freighters (#133316, #133322); trades at Deshima only in Shikoku (#133522);
  Polymer plant in the Border Worlds undercuts Samura (#133543, #133548); Honshu is "full of Kishiro people" (#133358).
  Founder's descendant works at Kansai (#133328). Probable funder of Blood Dragons per Liberty operatives (#133403).
- **GMG (gas miners guild)**: H-Fuel monopoly with Samura from Sigma-13 / Sigma-17 (#133316, #133339, #133346);
  fought Rheinland in the Eighty Years' War (#133339, #133347); "not truly Kusari anymore" (#133349); secretly
  funds and trains the Blood Dragons (#133341, #133347, #133427, #133437); about to switch to Kishiro miners (#133316,
  #133345); the police want the military to seal Sigma-13 (#133350). Ku04_05 GMG voice shoots Hogosha and Corsairs on
  sight (#133342) while another police voice notes the GMG now dislikes the Hogosha (#133353).
- **Others**: ALG (brings alloys from Dortmund, #133318); Republican, ALG, Kishiro, Samura, GMG all haul past Akita
  (#133354); Synth Foods (cheap Synth Paste, hated by the Farmers; Deshima food plant; IC-backed, #133481-#133483,
  #133583, #134841-#134842); IC (insurance, funds Synth Foods' hunters, #133470); Universal (a client of a Guild hunter,
  #133469); DSE (#133408).

### Criminal / insurgent
- **Blood Dragons**: see Threads. Royalist guard of Shogun Hideyoshi, fled the Sakura Coup of 321 AS (#133436); aim:
  end the bureaucrat-Samura-Hogosha bond and "return to New Tokyo victorious" after almost five centuries (#133424,

  GMG nebula fighting (#133437, #133347). Raid Fuchu to free prisoners (#133441), hit Nansei antimatter vessels
  (#133572), Kyushu food and Samura research (#133528), Chugoku Gate supplies (#133395, #133363, #133365), Tsushima
  (#133545).
- **Golden Chrysanthemums (GC)**: female revolutionary cult, Cardamine addicts; scripture "Golden Chrysanthemum in
  Bloom" and martyr Yuyu Matsuda (#133384, #133385); plan an empress chosen by Amaterasu, a seven-year-old girl being
  groomed (#133383, #133385); addiction as membership/security test (#133391); buy Cardamine from Outcasts at Cali,
  Tau-23 (#133391); land only at Roppongi (#133386); base Ainu Depot (#133378-#133392) and rumoured GC base west of
  Sapporo (#133421); raid Water (#133546, #133549), Fuchu (#133442), ram ships when doomed (#133466, #133492).
  Considered Blood Dragon allies by the press; Dragons disapprove (#133428, #133430, #133435).
- **Farmers Alliance (FA)**: unemployed Kyushu farmers fighting Synth Foods and Gaijin goods (#133534, #133585);
  base Kagoshima Depot (#133582-#133590); take orders from a "council of elders" and a patron in Shinjuku (#133584,
  #133590); sometimes hit Kishiro "by accident" (#133534, #133554); police call it a paper tiger (#133554).
- **Hogosha**: mafia-like, trade in Artifacts, bribe police, press intimidation for Samura (#133434), "connected"
  (#133469), ordered not to be pursued except for depot theft (#133353, #133531).
- **Corsairs / Outcasts (Edge)**: Corsairs raid Hiryo Cloud and Honshu (#133351, #133348, #133359, #134334); Outcasts
  attack Aomori via the Jump Hole from Sigma-19 (#133343), roam the Donryu Cloud (#133340), sell Cardamine at Cali
  (#133391); Hogosha / Corsairs cut GMG out of the Artifact trade (#133342).
- **Liberty Rogues / Lane Hackers / Xenos**: Rogue and Lane Hacker raiders, a hacker base in the Komatsu Cloud in
  Galileo (#133457); Xenos in Kepler hate outsiders (#133456, #133485).

## 5. Companies, commodities, technology
- **Gas mining / H-Fuel**: electrostatic field collectors pull Deuterium and Helium 3, Oxygen as by-product;
  Kishiro's miners can work thinner deposits (#133316, #133321). GMG-Samura monopoly of H-Fuel in Sigma-13 / 17
  (#133316). Samura wants Hokkaido gas as a monopoly breaker (#133412, #133432, #133407). Kishiro H-Fuel is
  advertised as faster fuel (#133476).
- **Superconductors / electrostatic heat shields**: Crow Nebula heat requires strong shields (#133331); Liberty's
  superconductors beat anything Kusari makes (#133332, #133570, #133575).
- **Antimatter**: primary-propellant ambition, on-the-fly generation, solar collectors, containment danger,
  metric tonnes stored at Nansei, Dragons target storage vessels (#133567-#133573).
- **Gate tech**: Kansai concluded gates and lanes are alien (#133332); stolen parts from criminal raids (#133337);
  gate-free jump battleship, prototype cruiser test against Kyoto, leap Kansai -> Sigma-17 within ten years
  (#133333, #133334, #133338); DSE guards parts (#133402, #133408).
