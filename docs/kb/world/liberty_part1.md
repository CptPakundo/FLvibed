## 1. Overview
Liberty (New York, California, Colorado, Texas, Alaska) seen from five angles: (a) the public news of the story's opening
weeks (Freeport 7 survivors, Schultzky's assassination, the Artifact ban, Orillion named and then reported dead, the
Kusari-Rheinland quarrel); (b) always-available local news about crime (Rogues, Junkers, Xenos, Outcasts, Lane Hackers),
the LPI police-and-prison business, Ageira, Planetform terraforming, the Navy; (c) criminal bar talk (Buffalo, Rochester,
Alcatraz, Ouray, Beaumont) about who ships what, and which jump holes and fields hide whom; (d) corporate bar talk in
Manhattan and Pittsburgh (Ageira, Bowex, Cryer, DSE, Samura, Synth Foods, Universal, Orbital, Republican, LPI, LSF, Navy,
Bounty Hunters); (e) KNOW vendors selling jump-hole locations, base "info" and wreck positions (topic lines only).
Picture: Liberty is rich and legalistic; a privatized police feeds a prison-factory economy; crime is layered and partly
symbiotic; after Freeport 7 the state (LSF, Navy) tightens security and the story's conspiracy leaks into the news.

## 2. Factions
### Lawful / state
- **Liberty government, President Jacobi**: Artifact ban (#15032, restated by LSF rumours #131235-#131238); battleships at
  every border gate (#15034); mediator in the Border Worlds talks between Kusari and Rheinland (#15012); promises the FP7
  findings will be shared (#15020). The Manhattan legislature ignores Colorado squatters (#56047).
- **LSF (Liberty Security Force)**, director **Aemon Bonnerille** (#15028, #15050, #15062, #15066): the government's "eyes and
  ears" (#131234); confiscates alien Artifacts, raids rich collectors, scolds Kusari for lax enforcement (#131235, #131236,
  #131241); hires cleared freelancers who must stay silent for life (#131237); Artifact interdiction is a top-down priority
  (#131238); cannot infiltrate the Outcasts (#131239); a Dark Ops Shadow Team was found dead, Cardamine stuffed in their
  mouths (#131240). Slow to question FP7 survivors (#15016, #15026); accused of silencing Orillion (#15066).
- **Liberty Navy**: best trained navy, budget sized for a war against two Houses (#131218); may not enter another colony's space
  without permission, hence Bounty Hunters (#131216); patrols the southern lanes where LPI will not go south of Detroit
  (#131215); Missouri patrols Zone 21 - Badlands (#131220); Badlands patrols have not found the Rogues' jump hole (#131221);
  Elite Fighter sold only to proven loyalists at Navy bases (#131214). Battleships named: Yukon (California, six-month refit
  at Norfolk, #56029), Rio Grande (bar brawl with Bounty Hunters, #56043), Mississippi (Rheinland says it protects poorly,
  #56063), Missouri. West Point hazing scandal (#56007).
- **LPI (Liberty Police, Inc.)**: privatized police acting on company policy (#131230); also a consumer-goods maker using
  prisoners as labour (#131229); runs the prison stations Sugarland and Huntsville; would be out of business without crime
  (#131215, #131233); accused of arresting people just to fill the prisons (#131233); 8th patrol wing at the Detroit field
  (#131222). Operations: Xenos in Detroit (#56011), squatters in Colorado (#56033), brawl in the Alamosa Field (#56039).
  Corruption: two officers bribed by a Junker, exposed by Free Eagle Press (#56013). Junkers hand over petty criminals to
  keep LPI off their backs (#131143); "Get Tough" bonuses push patrols deep into Pittsburgh debris (#56425); some officers like
  the Xenos (#131232); LPI "debris" slang for people spaced into the fields (#131231). Officers: Bill Watkins (#56003), Susan
  Davies (#56013), Mary Dennis (#56015), Sheri Henson (#56033).
- **Bounty Hunters Guild**: works for corporate contracts, hitman-like (#131291); many are ex-LPI prisoners (#131272);
  recruiting in Houston (#56055); two Guild Masters and six journeymen sent after Santiago Llorens (#56009); works with the
  Navy against Cardamine (#131217); "ghosted" Rogue smugglers (#56427).
- **IC**: corporate security / bounty payer; two million credits on Outcast Santiago Llorens (#56009), Badlands study
  (#56015), suspects Lane Hackers behind an Outcast gold hit (#56025). The chunk never spells the abbreviation out.
