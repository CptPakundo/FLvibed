## 1. Overview
Kusari lore here is seen through trade, grievance and corruption rather than through the main plot. Two diversified
mega-corporations split the empire: **Samura** (Kyushu farms, Junyo seafood, Yokohama shipyard, Osaka fuel, political
clout, the Hogosha as hired muscle) and **Kishiro** (Honshu factories, Shinagawa, technology, shipbuilding at Yukawa,
growing partnership with the gas miners). New Tokyo is neutral ground (#132906). Around them: a crime ecosystem
(Hogosha thieves/smugglers tolerated by police and employed by Samura; Blood Dragons = royalist terrorists; Golden
Chrysanthemums = feminist terrorists/Cardamine dealers; Farmers Alliance = Kyushu farmer militants fighting Liberty's
Synth Foods), foreign intruders (Liberty's IC at Roppongi, Republican Shipping and Bowex at Narita/Osaka, Synth Foods at
Deshima) and a deep economic resentment (Kyushu unemployment, land prices, Synth Paste vs Kyushu rice). The
**Hokkaido / Chugoku Gate** project is a huge, disputed money pit (said by many to be Samura's land grab for fuel
fields). The gas miners (GMG) are drifting from Samura toward Kishiro. The always-available text is a "peacetime" frame;
the **story-gated news** (#15162..#15224) shows the Kusari side of the Rheinland hostage crisis, the Artifact ban,
Tekagi's trip to Liberty and New Berlin, the outbreak of war and Tekagi's "murder".

## 2. Factions
### Lawful / state
- **Shogunate / Kusari government** (Shogun Edo): pressures Rheinland with Liberty and Bretonia (#15162), orders Tekagi to
  Liberty (#15164), declares open hostility after the Rheinland attack (#15204), fortifies the border and orders
  search/seizure of all ships in Kusari space (#15206), insists The Order killed Tekagi (#15220). Described as nearly
  bankrupt (arming the GMG in the Eighty Years' War, the Chugoku Gate burden: #132943). Blamed by IC for staging the
  Blood Dragon "enemy" to justify Hokkaido spending (#132962, #132984); by Kishiro for being Samura-friendly (#132888).
- **Governor Yoshinobu Tekagi**: see People.
- **Kusari Naval Forces**: ships built at Yokohama since 100 AS (#132940); Hokkaido is their "exclusive domain"
  (#132895), patrolled by battleship **Matsumoto** (#132946) and the Kyushu patrol by battleship **Nagumo**
  (#56213, #56215). Worried about Liberty's secret super-ship class beyond the Missouri (#132948). Uncertain about Samura,
  tempted by Kishiro's research and shielding (#132945). Contracts shrinking, bases run-down (#132954, #132951).
  Military Intelligence Bureau head: Admiral Nobu (#56217).
- **Kusari State Police** ("Junsa" officers, #132895, #132927): domestic police, hostile to foreign bounty hunters
  (#132893, #133287), post-by-post accounts of danger: Sapporo (Hokkaido) the worst post (#132895, #132927), Honshu
  clouds (#133279, #133280, #133282), Narita (#132924). Under orders to take no Blood Dragon prisoners so the convoys to
  Fuchu stay full (#133281). They have an understanding with the Hogosha: Artifacts yes, Cardamine no (#132889, #133003).
  Complaints that police follow Samura's wishes (#133276, #133285 inverse: Samura treats them as private security).
- **Gas Miners Guild (GMG)**: Kusari-descended nebula miners (Sigma systems), border guard for Kusari at low cost
  (#132946), bases at Aomori and Naha (#133270, #132976), Ogashawa joint station with Kishiro in Sigma-19 (#56229,
  #133274). Descended from the same ancestors as Honshu (#133278, #133301) but long estranged; seeking a homeland ocean
  planet in the Edge (#133301, #56289); youth leaving for Honshu (#56289, #56221). Feud with Samura over centuries-old
  gas prices (half the market price: #133300), Osaka talks deadlocked (#56227); GMG now negotiating with Kishiro
  (#133308, #132975); cut out of the Artifact trade by the Hogosha (#56371, #133005). "Miners Guild" appears as a
  different name for the same group (#56371).

### Corporations
- **Samura Industries**: HQ and research centre at Shinjuku Station (#132988); "largest corporation in the Sirius
  sector", vertically integrated from fertilisers to large transports (#132991); food (Kyushu, Junyo) and H-Fuel at
  Osaka; head Nora Samura (#56205). Controls Kyushu (#132977); owns Yokohama's commercial transport assembly, 70% of
  small/large transport market (#132951); is accused of: using the Hogosha to steal from competitors and foreign
  shippers (#132920, #132936, #132982, #132996, #133004), engineering Narita's law so foreigners land without interior
  storage (#132987, #132932), keeping Republican away from Yokohama (#132934, #132947), backing the Farmers Alliance
  (#132897, #132966, #132992), planning to sabotage/insure-claim the Osaka tanks (#133305), putting pressure on police
  patrols (#133276, #133308). Its own people admit the Hogosha arrangement (#132996, #133004) and the research on Alien
  Organisms (#132994). Samura rival in Honshu: loses Construction Machinery, Engine Components, Optronics to Kishiro
  (#133275, #133288). Declining under Kishiro's rise (#56231).
- **Kishiro Industries**: founded 91 AS by Katsuhiro Kishiro, "father of quantum optronics" (#132981). Centre of ops at
  Shinagawa Station for Sirius (#132973); Honshu factories (Optronics, Engine Components, Construction Machinery:
  #133273, #133275), Optical Chips (#132973), Gas Miners (#132979), Yukawa Shipyard (#133315, #56231), Kansai research
  (#132945). Left food production centuries ago (#132974); plans to quit cargo shipping and live off technology
  licensing like IC and Ageira (#132978). Pays for its own gates (Sigma-17) with the GMG (#132975). Accepts Synth Foods
  as inevitable (#132909, #133289); hires Honshu natives first (#56219). Works with Samura only reluctantly; accused of
  suspicious ties with the Blood Dragons because it is attacked less (#133293, #132894, #132915) and of Alliance attacks
  on its ships being puzzling (#132999).
- **Synth Foods** (Liberty): Synth Paste in Kusari-targeted flavours (#56179), cuts prices after Alliance attacks
  (#56185), shipping direct to Honshu from Deshima (#132909), plans 50 more biodomes at Deshima (#132908), won access to
  Honshu via a "real estate arrangement" on Deshima (#133294), got into Kusari because Manhattan offered a free loan
  for the Hokkaido gate in exchange for market access, as it did in Rheinland (#132900); wants to crack Kusari luxury
  seafood but Stuttgart can't farm it (#133296). Possibly linked to an investigation of Samura-Alliance links by IC
  (#132966).
- **Interstellar Commerce (IC)** (Liberty): Roppongi Station operator; Trade Lanes originally IC-funded (#132961);
  evicted from Kusari in the **Trade Lane Dispute of 521**, returned via a 710 amendment of the **Sirius Commerce Pact**
  (#132967); watches lanes in Kusari, Tau-29, Sigma-13 (#132968); disowns the Chugoku Gate (#132962); allows Golden C.
  to land (#132964); bars Bounty Hunters Guild members from Roppongi at Kusari's request (#132969); rents space to other
  Liberty firms (#132912). Spokesman John Wilkins (#56185).
- **Orbital Spa and Cruise Line**: runs the Hawaii liner (#56233, #132898, #132899); luxury food runs to Hawaii.
  Shuttle destroyed by Outcasts in the Chuyu Cloud (#133282).
- **Bowex** (Bretonia): carries Kusari/Bretonia freight over the Border Worlds since 258 (#132914); exclusive Bretonia
  right to import some goods directly from Kusari (#132916); sole foreign shipper allowed at Osaka for 200+ years,
  supplies all Bretonian H-Fuel (#133297); exclusive deal to move Kusari government MOX and Toxic Waste (#132939,
  #132949). Loses the Yukawa MOX contract to ALG (#133312). Warns of Osaka's dangerous tanks (#133304).
- **Republican Shipping** (Rheinland): can't land at Yokohama (#132934); moves Boron and High-Temperature Alloys from
  Mainz to Narita (#132934); filed a complaint on piracy at Narita (#56189). Considered respectable by a Bretonian
  speaker (#132913). Kusari bar-man thinks it should never have been let back after the war (#132929).
- Others: Universal (Liberty luxury goods; #132911, #132912), ALG (Ship Hull Panels, Alloys, MOX at Yukawa; #133312),
  BMM (rival of ALG on MOX; #133312), Gateway (Beryllium supplier, "sods" at Narita; #132913, #132919), LPI (Liberty

### Criminal / extremist
- **Hogosha** ("protectors"): Kusari's organised crime. Steals from storage depots at Narita and others (#132920,
  #132924, #132925, #132932, #132953), works openly for Samura (#133004), moonlights as Samura salarymen (#132985,
  #132924, #132982), employs the "Bosozoku" youths and kills the ones who break the no-drugs deal (#132985, #133003),
  runs the Kanto Field and the Kabukicho base (#132890), knows hidden Jump Holes used for Artifact smuggling (#132891,
  #132930, #133002, #133283), main trade: Alien Artifacts (#133005). Insists it is legitimate (#133000) and the
  "messengers" of a corrupt society (#133269). Politically active (#56367). Bosses: Ito Lei (#56177), Taki Fujimata
  (#56329); a Hogosha boss beaten to death with an Artifact (#56223); a disgraced underboss for Cardamine (#56369).
- **Blood Dragons** (royalist terrorists): want to restore the old order, revenge on the Hideyoshi Shogunate's fall
  (#56331, #133269). Hit Samura convoys hardest (#132915, #133285), pirate Kishiro only for supplies (#132894), hunted
  by State Police and Navy (#56217, #133281); strike from Kanto Field, Chiba Field, behind Kitadake (#132928,
  #132942, #132941), attack Tsushima's ice mines (#56215), the Hokkaido gate (#132941), Osaka (#133306, #133309); home
  at Kyoto Base in Chugoku (#56329, #56331, #56333). An alliance with the Golden C. is noted (#132944). Their own view
  of themselves: a "legitimate government" in waiting (#56331). Samura privately sees them as useful (#132995).
- **Golden Chrysanthemums** (GC, feminist terrorists/radicals): founded around the idea of a female leader of Kusari
  (#132959); "sisterhood" at Ainu worshipping Yu Yu Matsuda (#56407, #56409); supply Cardamine through Roppongi and
  recruit via it (#132960, #132933, #132889), popular with young women (#56201), Cardamine abuse up 200% since they began
  (#56201). Suicide-attack the Yokohama shipyard (#132944). Base area in Hokkaido (#132957), shield of IC at Roppongi
  (#132964). Hogosha cannot control them (#133003).
- **Farmers Alliance** (FA): Kyushu militants attacking Synth Foods transports (#56185, #132907, #132999, #133295),
  bombing the Deshima biodome (#56199, #56417); popular among older Kyushu people (#56419); backing gubernatorial
  candidate Hakita Toho (#56203). Samura "contributes" (#132992) and Hogosha sell them weapons (#133001); attacks also
  hit Kishiro for unclear reasons (#132999). Base: Kagoshima Depot (Ku03_05, inferred from #132997, #133001; KNOW at
  Kagoshima).
- **Bosozoku**: rowdy young gangsters hanging around Kabukicho (#132985, #133003).
- Foreign pirates in Kusari space: **Outcasts** (Chuyu Cloud, Tau systems, Honshu: #133271, #133282, #133280),
  **Corsairs** (Honshu, Sigma-13, Hiryo Cloud: #56225, #132965, #133314), **Rogues**, **Xenos** and **Lane Hackers**
  (Liberty/Independent side; #132881, #132884, #133268).
- **Bounty Hunters Guild**: only tolerated in Shikoku (Deshima); Kusari forbids them elsewhere (#132893, #132969).

## 5. Companies, commodities, technology
- **Trade geography**: Kusari trade lanes mostly via New Tokyo. Samura moves cargo across Sigma-13 to Rheinland,
  forced to transfer to Republican at Mainz (#132904). Narita is the foreign shippers' hub; Roppongi the Liberty hub
  (#132919, #132922, #132970). Foreign ships barred from Yokohama (#132887, #132934, #132972).
- **Commodities named**: Optical Chips, Optronics, Engine Components, Construction Machinery, Jump Gate Parts,
  Construction Supplies, Niobium, Beryllium, Gold, Boron, High-Temperature Alloys, Cobalt, Basic Alloys, Superconductors,
  Super Alloys, Polymers, Hydrocarbons, Ship Hull Panels, Scrap Metal, MOX, Toxic Waste, H-Fuel, Fertilizers,
  Luxury Goods, Luxury Food, Food (Kyushu Rice, Food Containers), Synth Paste, Pharmaceuticals, Side Arms, Liberty Ale,
  Cardamine, Alien Organisms, Alien Artifacts.
- **Artifacts**: the Hogosha's main business, demanded by rich New Tokyo and Manhattan elites (#133005); GMG used to be
  the middleman with the Corsairs, now bypassed via the Hawaii (#133005, #56371); a Liberty-ban makes the trade
  illicit and hurts dealers (#56181, #132882, #132892). Used as a murder weapon (#56223). Confiscated Artifacts are
  stored in Zone 21 (#15176).
- **Alien Organisms**: grown on Kurile for terraforming in Liberty and Bretonia and for feeding Junyo's fish farms,
  oxygenating its oceans (#132905, #132994, #132990). Workers sicken (#56191), farmed fish may harm health, kept secret
  (#132990).
- **Ships**: battleships Matsumoto and Nagumo; Missouri class and a rumoured new super class (#132948); Civilian Class
  (Yukawa, #56231); Gas Miners (Yukawa, Kishiro: #133315, #132979); Samura Small and Large Transports (#132951); luxury
  liner Hawaii (#56233).
- **Technology**: Kusari shielding and cloaking advanced during the Eighty Years' War (#132947); Kishiro has the
  better shielding (#132945); Kishiro studying Liberty's Trade Lane / Jump Gate technology; spy rumour that Liberty can
  now jump ships directly without static field generators (#132983); IC and Ageira profit from technology licences
  (#132978); jump gates cost: the Chugoku Gate "trillion credits" (#132955), Sigma-17 gates paid by Kishiro + GMG
  (#132975), Hokkaido gates paid by government for Samura (#132993).
- **Language/culture**: Gaijin (foreigner, #132880), Junsa (police), tanshin funin (separated worker, #132903),
  Bosozoku (gangsters), "the nail that sticks up gets hammered down" (#133269), Kusari palates and Kyushu foods
  (#132989, #133291). Beliefs: Kusari ancestors arrived on a sleeper ship from far away (#132886, #133284).
