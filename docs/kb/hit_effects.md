# Shield hits and weapon impacts: how they look (game data)

Generated by `tools\hitfx.js` from the install's DATA (st_equip.ini, weapon_equip.ini, effects.ini, DATA\FX [VisEffect] + .ale files via `tools\aledump.js`); do not edit by hand, re-run after an install change. Colours are the first -> last colour keys (additive blending unless noted: they light up, black = invisible); alpha / size keys are over the particle's life (0..1); life = the emitter's particle lifespan.

**What the frames showed (19 Oct 2026, `[game]` captures at ~150-185 fps, research\experience\shield_hits):** a hit on a shield draws BOTH the shield's effect (its family, below) and the weapon's impact effect; on a bare hull the weapon's stood out (the data also gives every ship a hull hit effect, a faint white ring + flare: `hull_damage.md`, not singled out in those frames). The damage tier (0 / 100 / 500 shield damage) changes only the SOUND for every family except Pirate (its three tiers have their own visuals) - all other tiers share one .ale effect.
