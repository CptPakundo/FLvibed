Subtitle Review

Keyboard shortcuts

Decide a line

1–5
select a read (or click it); the read the others agree with (= same text, ≈ same words) comes pre-selected
Enter
pick the selected read (or double-click, or Pick)
C
why it needed a change (optional): misheard, spelling, name, punctuation, other, none; saved with the pick
Shift+1–5
copy a read into the text box (Edit)
E
edit read 1 in the text box
Enter
in the text box: save, fitted to the conventions
Shift+Enter
in the text box: save exactly as typed
Esc
leave the text box
X
mark / clear "not sure" (Shift+X: with a note)
U · Ctrl+Z
undo the last save

Move

N · →
next line (skip)
P · ←
back, also to lines you saved

Listen

Space
play / pause from the playhead
R
replay from the start (a scene mix line: its window)
, · .
step the playhead 0.25 s back / forward (Shift: 1 s)
click
the timeline's empty track or its ruler: move the playhead there
F · G
the first / last half second of the speech
D
slow playback (0.75×) on / off
L
loop on / off
S
a chatter piece: play it in its sentence
A
same-text batch: play every ticked clip

Timeline (word timings)

click
a card plays from its word (while paused; it is selected too)
drag
a card moves it (with the selected ones), its edges trim it
Ctrl/Shift+click
select several (or drag on empty track)
A · Ctrl+A
select it and all after · select all
K
the selected word starts at the playhead (tap along)
Alt+←/→
nudge 10 ms (Shift: 100 ms)
[ ]
previous / next word
S
snap on / off
Ctrl+S
save the timings (Ctrl+Z undoes a change before)
W
show / hide the timeline
mouse
Ctrl+wheel zoom, wheel scroll, click the ruler to seek, double-click a card to play from it
dashed card = an estimate (not placed), orange = waiting for the aligner

stats
