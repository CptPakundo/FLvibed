## The fingerprint: p40 against p11e2 (6 Nov 2026, `tests\fingerprints\p40\`)
Frame order and `core_once` the same. Every other difference, named:
- **Hooks, five new:** `UiBatchClose` (the native HUD's frame at a load), `TractorNotify` and `QueueNN` (why the tractor refused: GitHub issue 33), `HudVertex` and `HudIndicatorDraw` (the targeting display over the game's bracket and label).
- **Import hook gone:** `common.dll!CreateID` from the subtitles, removed on purpose after p11e2 (5e37b14c): a research trace that only logged each hash, 2,400 lines with a flush at every load, and returned the game's own value. The game's hashing and the other readers of CreateID (`core\game\ai.cpp`, `hudinfo.cpp`, `baseinfo.cpp`) are unchanged.
- **Settings:** new `pad_sdl_fallback`, `hud_size`, `group_cue`, `skin_motion`, `skin_native`, `neural_net_reason`; `deck_mode` moved into `hud_size`. Reworded: `pad_backend`, `glyph_look`, `prompt_bar`, `game_text_size`, `hud_alerts`, `situation_shadow`, `situation_arbiter`. `rumble`'s default 60 to 100.
- **Commands:** the same hooks and keys in their output; the map of placed controls no longer lists its four `want ... placed` rows; the ini path is the worktree's. `ui_meta.h`'s hash follows the settings.
- **Perf: not taken.** The machine did not go quiet (RPCS3 at 3-6 cores against the limit of 3), so no perf file stands for p40 and the flight and skinned-screen budgets of section 10 are unmeasured for this build: the proof pass takes them on its first quiet window, else they stay recorded as not measured.
